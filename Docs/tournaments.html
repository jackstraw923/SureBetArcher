How these probabilities are calculated: Each tournament uses 50,000 Monte Carlo simulations of the full bracket. For NCAA events, team strength is measured by Pythagorean win percentage (points scored vs. allowed). For tennis, the Sackmann power-law Elo (exponent e=1.1, surface-adjusted) is used. The key distinction from simple head-to-head odds: the simulation propagates probabilities through the full bracket structure, capturing draw luck, seeding protection, and path-dependency effects that static models cannot see. Probabilities update each morning as results are processed and eliminated players are removed.
