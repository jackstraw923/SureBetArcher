WHO WE ARE

TRANSPARENT MATH.
NO TOUTS.

ArcVest is a quantitative sports market analysis platform operated under Mountain Capital, LLC. The engine driving the platform is developed and owned by Mountain Engineering. ArcVest is the consumer-facing interface to that engine.

The platform applies quantitative models to sports market pricing to identify positions where the model's probability estimate disagrees with the market's implied probability. Every position passes two independent mathematical filters — expected value and model edge — before it appears on the Daily Card. Position sizing uses fractional Kelly. Nothing passes on feel. Every position is supported by the math.

The track record is public from day one. No cherry-picked windows. No backtest theater. Every position committed at the morning seal is logged in the Performance Log with its entry price, model probability, edge, result, and dollar outcome. That record cannot be edited after settlement.

ArcVest is for informational purposes only. It is not financial advice and not betting advice. The platform's institutional claim is simple: transparent math over gut feel, portfolio discipline over cherry-picking, audit trail over promises.
