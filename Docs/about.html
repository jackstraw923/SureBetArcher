The Engine

How the Model Works

The ArcVest engine — called the SureBet Archer architecture internally — runs every morning to fetch live odds, compute model probabilities, apply the two-gate filter, and size positions using fractional Kelly. The process is fully automated and deterministic.

Sport-specific Pythagorean models compute win probability from points scored vs. allowed — PythagenPat for basketball, PythagenPuck for hockey, BaseRuns blend for baseball, and Elo ratings for tennis. These are the same underlying frameworks used in academic sports analytics research.

01
Odds Fetch — SureBetTarget
Live odds pulled from FanDuel, DraftKings, Hard Rock Bet, Fanatics, and ESPN Bet across 13+ sports every morning. Best-available line selected per market.
02
Model Probability — Sport Pipelines
Pythagorean win probability computed for each matchup. No-vig market probability stripped from the best available line. Both figures used in gate evaluation.
03
Two-Gate Filter — SureBetAim
Every trade must clear both gates independently: (1) model EV ≥ 5% and (2) positive model edge vs. no-vig probability. Most candidates fail. The survivors are the Daily Card.
04
Kelly Sizing — Portfolio Allocation
Quarter-Kelly criterion determines stake size. A 10% daily bankroll cap prevents any single session from dominating. Positions are sized proportionally to edge strength.
05
Settlement & CLV — SureBetAssessor
Results settled daily. Closing Line Value tracked to measure whether the model consistently beats the closing market price — the gold standard for evaluating edge sustainability.
