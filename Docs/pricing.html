Common Questions
What is the free trial?
14 days of full Pro access, no credit card required. You see everything — Kelly sizing, model probabilities, CSV exports. After 14 days you choose your plan or stay on the free tier.
Is this gambling advice?
No. ArcVest is a quantitative information tool. We identify positive expected value opportunities using mathematical models. Sports betting involves risk and past model performance does not guarantee future results.
How is Kelly sizing calculated?
We use quarter Kelly (Kelly/4) with a 10% daily bankroll cap applied uniformly across all qualifying picks. This is a conservative, portfolio-style approach — no single bet should dominate your bankroll.
How many picks per day?
Typically 40-80 qualifying picks across all sports after the two-gate filter. Volume varies by slate — heavy NCAAB days produce more picks; off-season days fewer. All picks clear the 5% EV minimum.
What's the model's track record?
Paper trading results are published publicly at arcvest.io/dashboard from day one. We don't cherry-pick results or run selective backtests. The full log is available to all users including free tier.
