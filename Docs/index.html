THE PROCESS

HOW ARCVEST WORKS

Three steps. No discretion. Every decision is traceable back to a number.

01
The model prices every game

ArcVest's quantitative model fetches market pricing across multiple sources each morning and builds no-vig fair probabilities. It then overlays sport-specific predictive models — such as Pythagorean win expectations for all major sports, BaseRuns for MLB, Elo and surface adjustments for tennis — to produce model win probabilities independent of the market.

02
Two-Gate filter for genuine edge

A position only qualifies if it clears both a minimum expected value threshold and a minimum model probability threshold. The market price alone is not enough — the model must independently agree. Positions that pass both gates appear on the Daily Card. The full market view, including positions below threshold, is always visible on the Odds page.

03
Kelly sizing. Sealed & tracked publicly.

Qualified positions are sized by fractional Kelly, capped by a daily portfolio exposure limit, and sealed every morning. The sealed record is immutable — it cannot be edited after the fact. Settlement runs each morning against final scores, and the result is appended to the public track record. No cherry-picking. No backtesting theater.

WHY THE MARKET CARES

PRICE CAPTURE IS THE
STRONGEST PROOF OF PROCESS

Outcomes are noisy. Any system can win over a small sample by luck. Closing line value — whether our entries beat the price the market eventually settled at — is the metric that is hardest to fake.

When ArcVest enters a position before the market fully reprices the information, that entry ages well. The closing price moves toward our number. That gap is Price Capture Alpha — skill extracted from the execution process, independent of whether the outcome wins or loses.
