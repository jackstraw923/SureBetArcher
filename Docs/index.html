The Engine

How ArcVest Works

01
Odds Fetch
Live odds pulled from 6 books across 13 sports every morning. Best-book selection for every market.
02
Pythagorean Model
Sport-specific win probability from points scored vs. allowed. PythagenPat for NBA/NCAAB, PythagenPuck for NHL, BaseRuns blend for MLB.
03
Two-Gate Filter
Every trade must clear both gates: minimum 5% expected value AND positive edge vs. no-vig probability. Most candidates fail. The survivors are real edge.
04
Kelly Sizing
Systematic Sizing: automated risk management ensures balanced portfolio allocation. No single trade dominates — positions scaled proportionally to edge strength.
